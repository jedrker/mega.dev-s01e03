The three problems

parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People follow the hint and type the month first; the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch it. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant have this cause.

oneDayMove: the date moves by one day. These reports say Tuesday became Monday, or that one meeting landed on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto. That is not a swapped month. It looks like a date shifted across time zones, and neither of the other fixes covers it.

Why one ticket hid three bugs

  • Triage merges on the subject line alone (decision 0001). “Wrong day on the invite” fits all three bugs, so they all landed in #4471.
  • Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. A day-first tenant’s staff on an en-US browser get tagged hint, and form reports get tagged parser. The table’s first line says how many tags are wrong.
  • Triage: duplicate reports and decision 0001 have not been reviewed since June 2024. The kb marks both stale.

What the desk does now

  1. Keep #4471 for the parser problem only, and link the other two tickets from it.
  2. File new date reports by cause, with the dedupe explorer on the triage page, not by subject.
  3. Do not mark #4471 fixed when the parser fix ships while the other two tickets are open.
